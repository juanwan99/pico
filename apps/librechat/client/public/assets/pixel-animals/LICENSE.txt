Pixel animal avatars are tiles from "Tiny Creatures" by Clint Bellanger
(https://opengameart.org/content/tiny-creatures), CC0 1.0,
compatible with Kenney Tiny Dungeon / Tiny Town (also CC0).

https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Selected tiles were nearest-neighbor scaled to 64×64 for chat avatars.
No original drawing; no avatar engine.
